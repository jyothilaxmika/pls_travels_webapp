How to Use Scrolling Panels
Panel Sizes:
  • scroll-panel-sm - Small panel (300px max height)
  • scroll-panel-md - Medium panel (400px max height)
  • scroll-panel-lg - Large panel (600px max height)
  • scroll-panel-xl - Extra large panel (800px max height)
  • scroll-panel-full - Full viewport height panel
Panel Variants:
  • scroll-panel-primary - Blue themed panel
  • scroll-panel-success - Green themed panel
  • scroll-panel-warning - Yellow themed panel
  • scroll-panel-danger - Red themed panel
  • scroll-panel-data - Optimized for tables and lists
  • scroll-panel-feed - Perfect for message feeds
Basic Structure:
<div class="scroll-panel scroll-panel-md">
    <div class="scroll-panel-header">
        Panel Title
    </div>
    <div class="scroll-panel-body">
        Your content here...
    </div>
</div>
