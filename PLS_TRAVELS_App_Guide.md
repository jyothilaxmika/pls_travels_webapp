## 👑 ADMIN USER GUIDE

### Dashboard Overview
Your admin dashboard provides a complete overview of all operations:
- **Fleet Statistics**: Total vehicles, active drivers, today's earnings
- **Multi-Branch Metrics**: Performance across Chennai, Bangalore, and other cities
- **Financial Summary**: Revenue, payments, and profit margins
- **Alert Center**: Important notifications and pending approvals

### 🏢 Branch Management
**Creating New Branches:**
1. Navigate to **Admin > Branches**
2. Click **Add New Branch**
3. Fill in branch details:
   - Branch name (e.g., "Chennai Central")
   - Contact information
   - Address details
   - Operating hours
4. Save to activate the branch

**Managing Existing Branches:**
- View all branch performance metrics
- Edit branch information
- Assign managers to branches
- Monitor branch-specific KPIs

### 👥 User Management
**Adding New Users:**
1. Go to **Admin > Users**
2. Click **Create User**
3. Select user role (Admin/Manager/Driver)
4. Enter personal details and credentials
5. Assign to appropriate branch (for Managers/Drivers)

**User Status Management:**
- **Active**: Full system access
- **Suspended**: Temporary access restriction
- **Terminated**: Permanent access removal

### 🚗 Fleet Management
**Adding Vehicles:**
1. Navigate to **Admin > Vehicles**
2. Click **Add Vehicle**
3. Enter vehicle details:
   - Registration number
   - Vehicle type (sedan, SUV, hatchback)
   - Brand and model
   - Insurance details
   - Emission compliance information
4. Upload required documents

**Vehicle Status Tracking:**
- **Available**: Ready for assignment
- **In Use**: Currently assigned to a driver
- **Maintenance**: Undergoing repairs
- **Out of Service**: Temporarily unavailable

### 💰 Financial Management
**Duty Scheme Configuration:**
1. Go to **Admin > Schemes**
2. Create new compensation schemes:
   - **Revenue Share**: 70/30 split between driver and company
   - **Fixed Daily**: Set daily guaranteed amount
   - **Trip-Based**: Per-trip commission structure
   - **Hybrid**: Combination of multiple models
3. Set Business Minimum Guarantee (BMG) if applicable

**Payment Processing:**
- Review driver earnings calculations
- Process manual payment adjustments
- Generate payment reports
- Handle advance payment approvals

### 📊 Reports & Analytics
**Available Reports:**
- **Daily Operations**: Driver performance, earnings, fleet utilization
- **Monthly Summary**: Branch-wise revenue, profit margins
- **Financial Reports**: Payment records, outstanding dues
- **Performance Metrics**: KPIs across all operations

### 🎯 Daily Duty Workflow

#### **1. Starting Your Duty**
1. Open the app and navigate to **Start Duty**
2. Select your assigned vehicle
3. Take required photos:
   - **Vehicle Exterior**: Front, rear, and both sides
   - **Odometer Reading**: Clear photo of current reading
   - **Fuel/CNG Level**: Current fuel gauge
4. Verify pre-filled information:
   - Starting odometer reading
   - CNG level from previous duty
   - Vehicle condition notes
5. Click **Start Duty** to begin GPS tracking

#### **2. During Active Duty**
- **GPS Tracking**: Your location is tracked every 30 seconds for safety and route optimization
- **Real-Time Earnings**: View updated earnings throughout the day
- **Photo Submissions**: Capture photos as needed for specific trips or incidents
- **Advance Requests**: Request advance payments through the app with location and reason

#### **3. Ending Your Duty**
1. Navigate to **End Duty** when ready to finish
2. Take required end-of-duty photos:
   - **Final Odometer Reading**: Clear photo showing total distance
   - **Final Fuel/CNG Level**: Remaining fuel/CNG
   - **Vehicle Condition**: Any new damage or issues
3. Fill in the earnings form:
   - **Cash Collected**: Total cash earnings
   - **Operator Bills**: Digital payment earnings
   - **Outside Earnings**: Non-app based income
   - **QR Payments**: UPI/QR code payments received
4. Add deductions if any:
   - **Pass Deduction**: Toll or permit fees
   - **Advance Deduction**: Any advance payments taken
   - **Fuel Expenses**: CNG or petrol costs
5. Submit for review and approval

### 💰 Understanding Your Earnings

#### **Earnings Calculation (Revenue Share Model)**
```
Total Income = Cash Collected + Operator Bills + Outside Earnings + QR Payments
Your Share (70%) = Total Income × 0.70
Deductions = Pass + Advance + Fuel Expenses
Net Earnings = Your Share - Deductions
```

#### **Alternative Schemes**
- **Fixed Daily**: Guaranteed minimum amount regardless of revenue
- **Trip-Based**: Fixed amount per completed trip
- **Hybrid**: Combination ensuring minimum guaranteed earnings

### 📸 Photo Requirements
**Quality Guidelines:**
- **Clear Focus**: Ensure all text and numbers are readable
- **Good Lighting**: Take photos in well-lit conditions
- **Complete Frame**: Capture the entire subject (odometer, vehicle, etc.)
- **No Obstructions**: Remove any items blocking the view

**Photo Types:**
- **Start Duty**: Vehicle exterior, odometer, fuel level
- **End Duty**: Final readings, vehicle condition
- **Incident**: Any damage, accidents, or unusual situations
- **Maintenance**: Service receipts, repair documentation

## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### **Login Problems**
- **Incorrect Credentials**: Contact your manager for password reset
- **Account Suspended**: Reach out to admin for account status
- **Network Issues**: Check internet connection and try again

#### **Photo Upload Issues**
- **Poor Image Quality**: Retake with better lighting and focus
- **Large File Size**: Use lower resolution if upload fails
- **Network Problems**: Photos will sync automatically when connected

#### **GPS Tracking Problems**
- **Location Permission**: Enable location access in browser settings
- **Poor Signal**: Move to an area with better GPS reception
- **Battery Saving**: Disable battery optimization for the app

#### **Earnings Discrepancies**
- **Missing Income**: Ensure all earnings sources are entered
- **Incorrect Deductions**: Review and adjust deduction amounts
- **Scheme Questions**: Contact manager for compensation clarification

### 📞 Getting Help
**Support Hierarchy:**
1. **Driver Issues**: Contact your branch manager first
